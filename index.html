What this is

A move-in packing checklist for a University of Alabama freshman, preloaded with about 130 items across eleven categories, from bedding and storage to the things nobody thinks about until the first week. Check items off as they go in a box, and add your own.

The list came from actually doing it rather than from a retailer's suggested cart, so it includes the unglamorous items and leaves out the ones that get bought and never used. Free, runs in the browser, nothing stored off your device.
